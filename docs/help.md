# Getting help

Work through these levels in order. It keeps the lab engineer's limited time for the problems only they can solve.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Level 0 · These docs**

    The page for your robot system: quick start, troubleshooting, examples. Use the search box at the top.

-   **Level 1 · System champion**

    A PhD or postdoc who knows the system well. Ask them for routine questions and small fixes. Names are on the [home page](index.md).

-   **Level 2 · Lab engineer**

    Only through the lab request list, never by direct message or in the corridor.

</div>

## What to put in a request

- Robot or system, with its ID
- What happens, and what you expected
- What you already tried (which Level 0 and Level 1 steps)
- Your PI
- Your deadline

!!! note "Larger work"
    A request that is development rather than support, for example a new feature or a new setup, needs approval from the lab coordinator before it goes on the list.
