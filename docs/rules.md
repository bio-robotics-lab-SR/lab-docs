# Lab rules

These apply to every system. System pages may add their own rules.

## Space and booking

- Floor and air space are booked in blocks in the lab calendar. A block is free if nobody has reserved it.
- Teaching blocks are entered at the start of each teaching period and take priority.
- Cancel a booking you won't use, so others can take it.

## Equipment

- Return robots switched off to their storage or charging place.
- Charge batteries only at the designated charging stations, never unattended overnight.
- Don't update firmware or system software yourself. Updates happen in the monthly update window.
- Broken equipment goes in the repair box with a note (ID and symptom), plus a request on the lab request list.

## Safety

!!! warning "Lithium batteries"
    A battery that is swollen, hot or smells odd goes out of use straight away. Switch the device off, unplug it, and tell the system champion.
